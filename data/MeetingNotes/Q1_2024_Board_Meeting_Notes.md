## 3. Customer Retention Crisis — Deep Dive

**CPO Isabelle Dumont** presented the churn analysis:

### Churn Metrics (Oct 2023 – Feb 2024)
| Metric | Oct 2023 | Jan 2024 | Feb 2024 | Target |
|--------|----------|----------|----------|--------|
| Monthly Churn Rate | 2.8% | 4.9% | **5.4%** | ≤1.5% |
| Net Revenue Retention | 97% | 91% | **87%** | ≥110% |
| LTV:CAC Ratio | 2.8x | 1.5x | **1.1x** | ≥3.0x |
| CSAT Score | 78 | 71 | **68** | ≥80 |

**Root cause analysis** (from 47 exit interviews):
1. **Integration complexity** (39% of churned accounts) — API v2 migration broke 14 enterprise connectors
2. **Support response time** (28%) — P1 ticket SLA dropped from 4h to 11h after team restructure
3. **Feature gap vs. competitor X** (21%) — real-time alerting and Slack integration missing
4. **Pricing** (12%) — annual renewal sticker shock for smaller accounts

### Immediate Actions Approved
- **Emergency Customer Success taskforce**: 5 CSMs moved from growth to retention effective March 18
- **API v2 migration support**: Engineering team to provide white-glove migration for 22 at-risk accounts
- **Support SLA restoration**: Hire 3 support engineers (approved budget: $180K)
- **Feature fast-track**: Slack integration and basic alerting scheduled for Q2 sprint

CPO Dumont: "If we execute on this, we project churn recovering to ~2.2% by June and NRR back to 98% by September."

## 4. Talent Update — CHRO Report

**CHRO Priya Nair** flagged early warning signals:

- Voluntary turnover: 18% TTM (industry benchmark: 12%)
- Time-to-hire for engineering roles: 47 days (target: 35)
- eNPS: +12 (down from +34 in Q2 2023)
- 9 open critical roles (3 senior engineers, 2 product managers, 4 in sales)

**Root causes identified:**
- Remote-first policy reversal (2-day office mandate) caused 4 voluntary exits in November
- Compensation benchmarks: P50 in 2022, now sitting at P38 vs market
- Career ladder not updated since 2021

**Proposed actions:**
- Compensation review using Radford 2024 data (estimated budget impact: $400K–$600K)
- Reverse 2-day office mandate → move to "intentional collaboration" model (team-led schedule)
- Publish updated career ladder by May 31

Board Chair Osei: "Let's not lose our engineering talent during a retention crisis — that's a compounding problem."

**Action Item**: CHRO to present comp review results May Board meeting.
