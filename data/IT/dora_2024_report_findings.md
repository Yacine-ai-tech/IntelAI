# DORA 2024 Report: Key Findings
Source: https://dora.dev/research/2024/dora-report/ (Google Cloud / DORA — Accelerate State of DevOps Report 2024)

## Main Discoveries

AI's Dual Impact: The report notes that "AI adoption significantly increases individual
productivity, flow, and job satisfaction," yet simultaneously creates negative effects on
software delivery stability and throughput. This underscores the importance of
fundamentals like small batch sizes and robust testing.

User-Centric Approach Drives Performance: Organizations focusing on end-user experience
build higher-quality products. Developers with this mindset demonstrate greater
productivity and satisfaction while experiencing reduced burnout.

Organizational Stability Matters: "Unstable organizational priorities cause meaningful
decreases in productivity and substantial increases in burnout." This impact proves
highly resistant to mitigation even in well-led environments.

Platform Engineering Benefits and Risks: Internal developer platforms improve
productivity and performance but can reduce change stability, requiring careful
implementation prioritizing developer independence.

Leadership's Role: Transformational leaders who inspire and support teams significantly
boost productivity, performance, and satisfaction while decreasing burnout.

Infrastructure Flexibility: Cloud adoption delivers performance gains only when
organizations embrace its inherent flexibility; migration without flexibility adoption
may prove counterproductive.

The report emphasizes experimentation and continuous learning for navigating
technological change successfully.
